11 of the 25 agents we hired returned nothing

Every listed agent on NEAR Agent Market is payable per call in USDC on NEAR. We hired the cheapest available ones on mainnet with real money, one at a time, and wrote down what came back. 3 more hires we cancelled ourselves before the agent's own deadline; they are listed and left out of the count. Nothing here was charged for a failure — the marketplace holds each payment until the agent delivers, and that part is exact. The cost is time: an agent that goes quiet burns its whole payment window before a buyer can be sure. We spent 8.5 hours waiting on hires that returned nothing, against 9.9 minutes of delivered work.

A buyer cannot tell the two apart beforehand. The catalog's own track record does not separate them: the agents below with the longest delivered-job histories are among those that never answered. Paying and watching is the only signal that holds, which is why AgentPay sells one — verified_route ranks x402 endpoints by whether a paid probe actually got data back. The marketplace already holds what it needs to show the same thing: delivery measured over paid hires, and the date an agent last delivered.

What the columns mean

What happened
delivered — the task completed and something came back. Any non-empty result counts, we do not grade it, so the share that returned nothing is a floor. no answer in the window — the agent accepted the job and never returned a result before the payment expired. had not started when we cancelled — the task was still waiting to start after the agent's listed deadline, so we cancelled; a cancelled task moves no money. rejected the task — the agent declined it. cancelled by us before its deadline — our sweep gave up on a task that had not started while the agent still had time; not counted either way. not reachable, not hired — the marketplace could not reach the agent, so no price was quoted and nothing was signed; not counted.
Charged
The marketplace holds the payment and settles it only on delivery, so everything other than a delivered hire cost nothing. That part works: the gap is between what the catalog advertises and what a buyer gets. Checked on chain on 14 Nov: the payer's USDC balance on NEAR fell by exactly $0.81 — $0.50 for the delivered hires, $0.01 for the direct purchase, $0.30 sent to Base through NEAR Intents.
