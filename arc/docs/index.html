What the columns mean

Settlement
gateway = Circle Gateway batched (an off-chain authorization, netted on-chain later; no per-call transaction). eip3009 = plain USDC transferWithAuthorization, one transaction per call.
Charged
The amount the signed authorization carried; "no charge found" when neither a receipt nor an independent settlement record exists for it. Flagged when it differs from the price in Circle's Agent Marketplace catalog; the live 402 challenge is recorded in the JSON.
Unpaid → paid
Status and latency before any payment, then after paying. A non-402 on the left means the provider validated or served the request before asking for money; a "!" on the right carries the provider's error.
Delivered
A 2xx with a non-empty, non-error body after payment.
Receipt
Whether the provider returned a PAYMENT-RESPONSE settlement receipt with the data. "settled" means the receipt was missing but the probe found the charge independently — in Arc's transfer logs (eip3009) or Circle Gateway's transfer ledger (gateway).
Proof
What the buyer can point at afterwards: an Arc transaction, a Gateway transfer id, or nothing.
Anchored
The probe writes a hash of each receipt to the ReceiptAnchor contract on Arc, so the row can be checked later even when settlement itself left no transaction.

Shape only: response bodies are never published, only their size and hash. Source and method: github.com/romudille-bit/agentpay/arc.
